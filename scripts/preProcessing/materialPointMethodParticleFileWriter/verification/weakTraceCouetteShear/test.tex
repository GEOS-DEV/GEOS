\paragraph{Problem.}
This verification problem shears a viscous fluid layer between elastic platens
in a periodic-in-$x$ plane-strain domain.  The lower platen is fixed and the
upper platen is driven with a prescribed tangential velocity; the two
platen-fluid interfaces are the explicit weak-trace or cohesive-zone surfaces.

\paragraph{Expected response.}
For bonded no-slip Couette shear the tangential velocity is continuous at each
platen-fluid interface.  The fluid velocity should approach the linear Couette
profile between the fixed lower platen and moving upper platen, and the weak
trace variant should transfer tangential motion without interface slip or
opening.
