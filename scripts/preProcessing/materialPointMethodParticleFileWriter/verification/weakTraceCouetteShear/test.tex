\paragraph{Problem.}
This verification problem shears two elastic layers between parallel plates in a
periodic-in-$x$ plane-strain domain.  The lower and upper plates move with equal
and opposite tangential velocities, and the horizontal material interface is the
only explicit weak-trace or cohesive-zone surface.

\paragraph{Expected response.}
For bonded no-slip Couette shear the tangential displacement is continuous at
the interface and the shear traction is continuous across the two materials.
The layer shear strains are inversely proportional to their shear moduli.  The
weak-trace variant should reproduce the stiff elastic cohesive-zone reference
without interface slip or opening.
