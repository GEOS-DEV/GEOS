\paragraph{Problem.}
This verification problem places a circular elastic inclusion at the center of a
linear elastic matrix disk and applies a slow virtual-fluid pressure ramp to the
outer curved material boundary.  The material interface is curved and
intentionally cuts through mixed grid cells.

\paragraph{Expected response.}
The bonded concentric inclusion solution has a continuous radial displacement
and continuous radial traction across the interface, with piecewise radial and
hoop stresses determined by the matrix and inclusion elastic constants.  The
weak-trace variant should reduce mixed-cell stress artifacts without creating
interface slip or opening relative to the stiff elastic cohesive-zone reference.
