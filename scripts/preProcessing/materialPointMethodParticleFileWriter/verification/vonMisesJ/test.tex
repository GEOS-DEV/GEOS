\subsection{Von Mises uniaxial plasticity}
\label{sec:verification:von-mises-uniaxial}

This test isolates the \texttt{VonMisesJ} constitutive update and equivalent plastic-strain output.  A single cube is stretched monotonically in $y$ to a small final strain with lateral faces released, producing an approximately one-dimensional elastic-perfectly-plastic stress path.

\begin{figure}[h]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.72\linewidth]{\CaseSourceDir/setup_von_mises.png}
  \caption{Von Mises uniaxial loading setup.  The measured box-average axial stress is compared with the ideal elastic-perfectly-plastic law.}
\end{figure}

The analytical comparison uses
\begin{equation}
  \sigma_y^{\star}(\epsilon)=\min(E\epsilon,\sigma_0),\qquad
  \epsilon_p^{\star}=\max\left(0,\epsilon-\frac{\sigma_0}{E}\right),
\end{equation}
with $E=1$ and $\sigma_0=0.02$.  The metric is RMS and maximum stress error; equivalent plastic strain is also reported when present in the history output.
