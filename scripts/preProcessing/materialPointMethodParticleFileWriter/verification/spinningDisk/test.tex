\subsection{Spinning-disk conservation}
\label{sec:verification:spinning-disk-conservation}

This test isolates particle-grid transfer conservation and dissipation behavior.  A two-dimensional elastic disk is initialized in solid-body rotation and then evolves without external forces.  The same input is dispatched for PIC, FLIP, and FMPM2 variants.

\begin{figure}[h]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.72\linewidth]{\CaseSourceDir/setup_spinning_disk.png}
  \caption{Spinning-disk setup.  Tracer velocities define angular-momentum and kinetic-energy proxies that should remain constant for the ideal force-free motion.}
\end{figure}

The tracer-based diagnostics are
\begin{equation}
  L_z=\sum_i (x_i v_{y,i}-y_i v_{x,i}), \qquad
  K=\frac{1}{2}\sum_i \left(v_{x,i}^2+v_{y,i}^2\right).
\end{equation}
The metrics are maximum and final relative drift in $L_z$ and $K$.
